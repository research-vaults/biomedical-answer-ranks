# Data, attribution and rights boundaries

- **MedEinst**, Chen et al., *MedEinst: Benchmarking the Einstellung Effect in Medical LLMs*: https://huggingface.co/datasets/zhui711/MedEinst and https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06636. The dataset card declares **CC BY 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The distributed case text is a selected subset with experimental prompts, proposals and scoring annotations added. These additions are not endorsed by the original authors. The card describes synthetic counterfactual narratives grounded in DDXPlus; this package does not distribute a private patient dataset or the full DDXPlus knowledge base. Original case IDs and branch identity are retained for traceability.
- **MedXpertQA**, Zuo et al., *MedXpertQA: Benchmarking Expert-Level Medical Reasoning and Understanding*, ICML 2025: https://github.com/TsinghuaC3I/MedXpertQA and https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18362. The repository and README specify **MIT**. The used 128 text questions/options and official keys are included in the transport configuration and derived requests; images and the full benchmark are not bundled. The required copyright/license is in `docs/MEDXPERTQA_LICENSE.txt`. Retaining the source's official key is not independent clinical validation.
- **Terminology metadata:** small diagnosis-name/code extracts and source locators support the reported scoring audit. MeSH and CDC identifiers refer to public terminology, not a new clinical gold standard. Full third-party terminology archives and repositories are excluded. Public source locators and citations are retained where needed to interpret the mapping decisions.
- **NeurIPS style:** the distributed `neurips_2026.sty` is the unchanged official style used by the manuscript. Its third-party names/contact comments are retained as attribution, not presented as this paper's authors or affiliations. No third-party license notices have been stripped.
- **Generated observations:** candidate diagnoses, brief rationales, opaque candidate identifiers, selected IDs, prompt settings, completion reasons and numerical annotations are research outputs. Provider account identifiers, raw provider envelopes, operational addresses and billing infrastructure are not included. Aggregate reported costs and token counts are retained only where scientifically useful.

Licenses for MedEinst and MedXpertQA were rechecked against their public source pages on 2 October 2026. Privacy is not used as a substitute for redistribution permission. This document does not grant new rights to any third-party data, the manuscript or cited papers. No copies of comparator papers or cloned repositories are distributed.

- **MMLU-Pro Health:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/MMLU-Pro declares MIT for the dataset. This release's replay inputs contain native answer IDs, keys and generated ranks, not the benchmark's Health question text. The separate upstream code repository uses Apache-2.0; its code is not copied here. Dataset identifiers and pinned revisions are retained inside the replay metadata. Neither source's license is silently applied to the other's materials.
- **RankedVotingSC:** the tie-policy capsule contains one unmodified MIT-licensed `RankBasedSC.py` from szu-tera/RankedVotingSC at commit `f5bb1c1afcee31d2156ca68d441d47ee52c70f18`, together with its license notice. This is a necessary aggregation reference component, not a bundled third-party repository.
