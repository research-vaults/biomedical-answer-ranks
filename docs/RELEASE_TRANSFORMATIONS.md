# Data transformations and reproduction scope

## Observations and provenance

Retained records include returned answer text, parsed decisions, failed attempts,
completion reasons, usage and experimental settings when recorded. Provider
account envelopes and operational identifiers are omitted. No missing answer is
filled in by the export. Large-file manifests describe stored shards and their
reconstructed logical streams separately.

Portable paths replace original machine locations. Scientific seeds, scoring
versions and hash namespaces remain unchanged because they affect sampling and
ordering. Checksums marked `release_integrity_only` identify the exported bytes;
they are not retrospective preregistrations. Frozen experimental protocols and
amendments inside the replay capsules retain their scientific timing and scope.

## Analyses

`reproduce_latest.py` runs the ten current capsules, including native-key
representation and selected-policy comparisons, Health transfer and exploratory
robustness analyses. Each capsule describes its population, uncertainty,
expected outputs and limitations. Saved negative and partial results are not
newly generated evidence.

The earlier `reproduce.py` reconstructs support-channel and clinical-mapping
controls. It checks retained numerical targets and regenerates their development
plots. Earlier clinical scoring depends on the frozen candidate maps; terminology
code is supplied in `src/terminology_reference/`, but rebuilding that authority
also requires its named external terminology inputs. Numerical agreement does
not independently validate clinical labels.

The current manuscript's figure assets and complete sources are supplied.
Building the paper uses those assets; the numerical replay does not regenerate
every original current-figure plotting pipeline. Optional new request execution
is a new stochastic run, not recovery of historical provider kernels or outputs.

## Version notes

The distributed PDF and extracted scientific source are the unchanged 1 October
2026 research draft. The source ZIP contains the same source files. Older wording
inside frozen capsule READMEs about internal creation describes their original
preparation, not the present repository's public visibility. Administrative
publication receipts are not scientific evidence and are not needed to run the
analyses.
